Reset your day

Days don't always go to plan. That's not failure — that's ADHD. Three questions to find your footing again. No pressure.

Question 1 of 3

Not the most important — the most possible right now.
Question 2 of 3
What will you do in the next 30 minutes?
One action. Specific. Small enough that starting feels easy.
Question 3 of 3
What's a 10-minute version of your original plan?
